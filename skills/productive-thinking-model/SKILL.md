# Productive Thinking Model

Turn the user's problem into a target future, success criteria, catalytic questions, a forged solution, and an action plan with owners.

## Input

Work from the user's context. Infer what you can and state assumptions. Ask only for gaps that block a correct plan (one round, max 3 questions). Typical gaps: constraints, available resources, who is involved.

## Steps

1. **What's going on?** Describe the problem, its impact, what is known, who is involved, and the **target future** (what the world looks like when the problem is solved).
2. **What's success?** Write success criteria with DRIVE:
   - **Do:** what must the solution do?
   - **Restrictions:** what is the solution not allowed to do? (Use inversion.)
   - **Investment:** what resources can we spend?
   - **Values:** what qualities must it have?
   - **Essential outcomes:** what result is non-negotiable?
3. **What's the question?** Write 3-6 catalytic questions ("How might we...?") that, once answered, reach the target future. Mark the 1-2 most critical.
4. **Generate answers.** For the critical questions, brainstorm 8+ ideas without judging. Include unusual ones.
5. **Forge the solution.** Score the top ideas against the DRIVE criteria (a small decision matrix). Pick the best, then strengthen it: what makes it fit the criteria better?
6. **Align resources.** List actions, owners, resources, and dates. Done when every action has an owner.

## Output

```markdown
## 1. What's going on
<problem, impact, knowns, people> - **Target future:** <...>

## 2. Success (DRIVE)
- Do: ...
- Restrictions: ...
- Investment: ...
- Values: ...
- Essential outcomes: ...

## 3. Catalytic questions
- **How might we <...>?** (critical)

## 4. Ideas
- <idea>

## 5. Forged solution
| Idea | Do | Restrictions | Investment | Values | Outcomes | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
**Chosen:** <solution, with improvements>

## 6. Action plan
| Action | Owner | Resources | Due |
|---|---|---|---|
```
